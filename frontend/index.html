Categories & Information Guide

The Intake Agent extracts these 9 categories from the text:

  • Name: Full name of applicant.
  • Course: Degree & level (must state undergraduate to pass rule E3).
  • Latest result: Examination percentage (must be ≥ 60% for rule E1; ≥90% earns 100 merit pts).
  • Family income: Annual rupees (e.g. Rs 3,50,000 or 4 lakh; ≤ 8,00,000 for rule E2).
  • Dependents: Number of family dependents (3-4 earns 70 need pts; 5+ earns 100 pts).
  • Other financial aid: Existing aid percentage (0% or none earns 100 need pts).
  • Documents: Must include both income certificate and marksheet (rule E4).
  • Achievements: Formatted as National: ..., State: ..., or Institute: ...
  • Statement: 50-250 words covering career goals, quantitative numbers, and community impact.
